Privacy policy

In effect from 6 October 2026

Night Falls on Palermo collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. No account, no ads, no tracking.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps three things, on the phone only:

  • The players of the current game.
  • The current deal: who got which role, and who has looked.
  • The names saved from earlier games, up to 60.

Nothing else. No location, no contacts, no device details.

Where it goes

Nowhere. The app does not send your data to anyone, not even its developer.

The “Support” link opens a web page in your browser. That page, like the web version of the app, is a set of plain files on a web host, and the host may keep ordinary visit logs.

Removing it

  • Hold “Clear list” to remove the players and the deal.
  • Hold a saved name to forget it, or hold “Forget all names” to forget them all.
  • Delete the app and everything is gone.

Backups

A backup of your iPhone can include the app’s data. That backup is between you and Apple. On Android the app is left out of backups.

Children

The app is not aimed at children, and it collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text will be here with a new date.
