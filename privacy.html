Privacy policy

In effect from 26 October 2026

Night Falls on Palermo keeps your names and games on your phone and sends them nowhere. The iPhone and Android apps show ads, which come from Google.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps five things, on the phone only:

  • The players of the current game.
  • The current deal: who got which role, and who has looked.
  • The game: who is out, and who the Doctor saved each night.
  • Your choices in Settings.
  • The names saved from earlier games, up to 60.

In a secret vote the votes are counted, then forgotten. Who voted for whom is never kept.

The game needs nothing else: no account, no location, no contacts, no microphone.

Where it goes

Nowhere. The app does not send your names or your games to anyone, not even its developer.

The “Support” link opens a web page in your browser. That page, like the web version of the app, is a set of plain files on a web host, and the host may keep ordinary visit logs.

Ads

The iPhone and Android apps show an ad once everyone has seen their role, and between games. The web version has none.

The ads come from Google (AdMob). To show them, Google gets from your phone:

  • Its advertising ID.
  • Its network address, which tells roughly where you are.
  • Its model, its system version and its language.
  • Which ads you saw, and which you tapped.

Google never gets the names or the roles. They stay on the phone.

In the EU, the UK and Switzerland the app asks you first. You can change your answer whenever you like: “Ad choices”, at the foot of the Rules.

On an iPhone, iOS also asks whether the app may track you. If you say no, you still see ads. They are just not chosen for you.

How Google uses what it gets: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

The purchase

“Remove ads” in Settings is a one-time purchase. You pay through the App Store or Google Play, and the app never sees your payment details. It only learns that the purchase was made.

After it, the app shows no ads and asks Google for none.

Removing it

  • Hold the clear-list icon at the top of Players (“Clear list”) to remove the players, the deal and the game.
  • A new deal removes the game, and so does “Play again”. A game that has ended stays until then.
  • Hold a saved name to forget it, or hold the bin by the saved names (“Forget all names”) to forget them all.
  • Delete the app and everything is gone.

What the ads are about is with Google, not on your phone. You can reset your advertising ID in the phone’s settings.

Backups

A backup of your iPhone can include the app’s data. That backup is between you and Apple. On Android the app is left out of backups.

Children

The app is not aimed at children. It asks Google for no ads that are for adults only.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text will be here with a new date.
