{{app: app.name}} is an app for iPhone and Android that deals the roles of the party game it is named after, Palermo for short. It is made by {{developer}}. This page says what the app does with information, and what this website does.

The short version

The app collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and it makes no network requests. What the players type is kept by the app on the phone it was typed on, until you remove it.

What the app stores, and where

The only information the app has is what the people at the table type and choose. Three of the things it keeps hold names:

  • the list of players for the game you are setting up;
  • the current deal: the same names, the role each one was dealt, and whether they have looked at it;
  • the saved names: everyone who was dealt a card in an earlier game on this phone, so that next time nobody is typed again. A name is saved when a game is dealt with it. The saved names are names only, never a role, and the app keeps 60 of them at most.

It also keeps the roles you picked, the language, and whether the extra roles are shown.

All of it is kept in the app’s own storage on the device. The app does not send it to the developer or to anyone else, and there is no server it could send it to.

Removing it

Everything is removed on the {{app: players.title}} screen, with a hold of one second:

  • “{{app: players.clear}}” removes the list of players and the current deal. The saved names stay.
  • Holding a name under “{{app: players.saved}}” forgets that name. The names shown there are the saved names that are not in the list.
  • “{{app: players.forgetAll}}” forgets all the saved names. The list and the current deal stay.

To take every name off the phone, hold “{{app: players.clear}}” first and then “{{app: players.forgetAll}}”. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

What the app does not have

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No analytics and no crash reporting.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifier.
  • No purchases.
  • No third-party SDK that collects data.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or notifications.
  • No network requests of any kind.

On Android the app declares two permissions. The app is drawn by Android System WebView, a component of the system, and network access is declared so that it can load the app’s own pages, which are inside the app itself; the app contacts no server. Vibration is for the short tick when a card turns.

At the foot of its rules page the app has two links to this website: to this policy and to the support page. They open in your phone’s browser, and only when you tap them. The app itself still requests nothing.

Backups made by your phone

A backup made by a phone’s operating system can include the data of the apps on it. On iPhone, an iCloud or computer backup of the phone can therefore contain what the app stored, the names included. That backup is yours: it is kept in your own iCloud account or on your own computer, and the developer has no access to it.

On Android, backup and transfer to a new device are switched off for this app, so they do not include what it stored.

This website

The site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, has no analytics and no forms, and loads nothing from third parties. It keeps one thing in your browser: the language you chose with the language switch, so that the choice is remembered. That value stays in your browser.

The service that hosts these pages may keep the ordinary logs of a web server, such as IP addresses and the pages that were requested. The links to the App Store and to Google Play lead to Apple and to Google, which have policies of their own.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, of any age.
