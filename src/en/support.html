Questions

I forgot my role. How do I see it again?

On the Table, tap your name. Tap “{{app: reveal.thatsMe}}” and hold the card again.

Someone was away when the phone went around.

On their turn, tap “{{app: reveal.skip}}”. On the Table their name then says “{{app: table.notLooked}}”. When they are back, tap their name.

How do I change the players?

On the Table, tap “{{app: table.changePlayers}}”. Add or remove names, then deal again. The phone goes around in the order of the list.

Do I type the names again next time?

No. The phone saves every name a game was dealt with. On the {{app: players.title}} screen, the saved names that are not in the list stand under it, under “{{app: players.saved}}”. Tap a name to add it.

How do I forget a saved name?

On the {{app: players.title}} screen, under “{{app: players.saved}}”, hold the name for one second. To forget all of them, hold “{{app: players.forgetAll}}”. A name that is in the list is not shown there: take it out of the list first.

How do I change the roles?

On the Table, tap “{{app: table.changeRoles}}”. Switch roles on or off, or take the ones the app suggests, then tap “{{app: roles.deal}}”. The Doctor, the Paranoid and the two Kamikazes are under “{{app: roles.advanced}}”. The app asks for two things only: at least one Killer, and no more roles than players.

What do Red and Black mean?

The Red Killer and the Black Killer pick the victim together. The Snitch knows the Red Killer. Nobody else knows the Black Killer.

For a Kamikaze, Red means day and Black means night. The Red Kamikaze takes one player along if voted out by day. The Black Kamikaze takes one along if killed at night. Both Kamikazes are with the town.

Who is the Paranoid with?

With nobody. The Paranoid plays alone, and wins the game if voted out by day.

I tap the button and nothing happens.

“{{app: table.playAgain}}”, “{{app: table.revealAll}}”, “{{app: players.clear}}” and “{{app: players.forgetAll}}” need a hold of one second, not a tap. That way nothing is dealt, shown, cleared or forgotten by accident.

How do I switch between Greek and English?

At the top of the Home screen: EN | ΕΛ. The players and the deal stay as they are.

Where are the rules?

Under “{{app: home.rules}}” on the Home screen, and behind the book icon at the top of the Table.

Does it need internet?

No. The app works offline and needs no account. Only the two links at the foot of the rules open in your browser: the privacy policy and this page.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later, which means iPhone 8 and newer. Android 7 or later with an up-to-date Android System WebView (version 111 or newer). If the app opens on a page that asks for an update, update “Android System WebView” from Google Play and open the app again. On Android 7, 8 and 9, update “Chrome” instead.

How do I remove the names from the phone?

On the {{app: players.title}} screen, hold “{{app: players.clear}}”. It removes the list and the current deal. The saved names stay: hold “{{app: players.forgetAll}}” to remove those too. Deleting the app removes everything. More in the privacy policy.
