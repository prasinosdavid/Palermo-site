How it works

  1. {{shot.players: The Players screen: eight names in a list, and the button “Continue · 8 players”.}}

    Add the players

    Add everyone playing, the narrator too. From 4 to 30 players. The phone remembers the names for next time.

  2. {{shot.roles: The Roles screen: a switch beside each role.}}

    Pick the roles

    Or take the ones the app suggests. Citizens fill the rest.

  3. {{shot.hold: A role card held face up: the Doctor, with the line “Saves one player each night.”}}

    Pass the phone around

    Each player holds the card to see their role. Let go and it hides.

  4. {{shot.table: The Table screen: the eight names and no roles, above the button “Play again”.}}

    Put it down and play

    For another game, hold “{{app: table.playAgain}}”.

What the app does not do

It deals the roles and shows the rules. It does not narrate, keep time, count votes or name a winner. You play the game at the table.

Privacy

{{app: about.privacy}} It works offline, with no account and no ads.

It keeps three things on your phone: the list of players, the current deal and the names saved from earlier games. Hold “{{app: players.clear}}” to remove the list and the deal. Hold a saved name to forget it, or hold “{{app: players.forgetAll}}” to forget them all. Deleting the app removes everything.
