Deals the roles

For Palermo, the Greek take on Mafia. One phone for the whole table.

  • Coming soon to the App Store
  • Coming soon to Google Play

How it works

  1. Add the players

    Add everyone playing, the narrator too. From 4 to 30 players. The phone remembers the names for next time.

  2. Pick the roles

    Or take the ones the app suggests. Citizens fill the rest.

  3. Pass the phone around

    Each player holds the card to see their role. Let go and it hides.

  4. Put it down and play

    For another game, hold “Play again”.

The nine roles

The town, the killers, and one who plays alone.

Town

  • Citizen Find the killers.

  • Policeman Knows the Snitch.

  • Doctor Saves one player each night.

  • Red Kamikaze Voted out? Takes one player along.

  • Black Kamikaze Killed at night? Takes one player along.

Killers

  • Red Killer Kills at night.

  • Black Killer Kills at night.

  • Snitch Knows the Red Killer.

Alone

  • Paranoid Wants to be voted out.

What the app does not do

It deals the roles and shows the rules. It does not narrate, keep time, count votes or name a winner. You play the game at the table.

Privacy

The app collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. It works offline, with no account and no ads.

It keeps three things on your phone: the list of players, the current deal and the names saved from earlier games. Hold “Clear list” to remove the list and the deal. Hold a saved name to forget it, or hold “Forget all names” to forget them all. Deleting the app removes everything.
