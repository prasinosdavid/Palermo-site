Questions

I forgot my role. How do I see it again?

On the Table, hold your name. Your card comes up while you hold it, and goes when you let go.

Does the phone go around in the order of the list?

No. Pass it in the order you sit. Each player holds their own name on the Table until the card turns over, lets go, and passes the phone on.

What does the eye with a number mean?

How many times that card was turned. Each player looks once. A card seen more often was looked at again: by its player, or by someone else.

How do I change the players?

On the Table, tap “Change”. Add or remove names, then tap “Deal”.

Do I type the names again next time?

No. The phone saves every name a game was dealt with. On the Players screen, the saved names that are not in the list stand under it. Tap a name to add it.

How do I forget a saved name?

On the Players screen, under the list, hold the name for one second. To forget all of them, hold the red bin over them (“Forget all names”). A name that is in the list is not shown there: take it out of the list first.

How do I change the roles?

On the Table, tap “Change”, then the roles over “Deal”. Tap a role to switch it on or off, or tap the magic wand for the roles the app suggests, then tap “Deal”. All nine roles are on one screen. The app asks for two things only: at least one Killer, and no more roles than players.

How do I turn the narration off?

Tap the gear at the top of the Table to open Settings, and turn “Voice” off. The phone then plays no sound. Each line shows on screen with “Next”, for one of you to read out.

Can we play without the phone running the game?

Yes. In Settings, choose “Roles only”. The app then deals the roles and shows the rules, and you play the rest at the table, with one of you as the narrator.

The phone is on silent. Will we hear the narrator?

Yes. On an iPhone the silent switch does not mute the narrator. Set the volume with the side buttons while the app is open, on Android too. If you still hear nothing, check that “Voice” is on in Settings.

The phone rang in the middle of the night.

The night stops when the app is left, so nobody sees a list they should not. Back in the app, tap “Night falls”: that night starts again from the top. Nothing of it was kept.

The Doctor is out, but the phone still calls them.

On purpose, when nobody saw their card. If the Doctor died at night and the phone skipped them, everyone would know. So it waits as long as a Doctor might think, then goes on. A Doctor voted out, or taken along by the Red Kamikaze, showed their card: the phone stops calling them.

What do Red and Black mean?

The Red Killer and the Black Killer pick the victim together. The Snitch knows the Red Killer. Nobody else knows the Black Killer.

For a Kamikaze, Red means day and Black means night. The Red Kamikaze takes one player along if voted out by day. The Black Kamikaze takes one along if killed at night. Both Kamikazes are with the town.

Who is the Paranoid with?

At first, with nobody. Voted out on the first day, or killed on the second night, the Paranoid wins the game. Otherwise, they’re with the town.

I tap the button and nothing happens.

“Play again”, “All roles” and the red icons that clear the list and forget the names need a hold of one second, not a tap, and so does every name you pick or vote out in the game. A short tap only says “Press and hold”. That way nothing is dealt, shown, cleared, forgotten or decided by accident.

How do I switch between Greek and English?

At the top of the Home screen: EN | ΕΛ. The players and the deal stay as they are. The narrator speaks the language of the app.

Where are the rules?

Behind the book icon at the top of the Home screen, and of the Table.

How do I remove the ads?

Open Settings with the gear at the top of the Home screen, and tap “Remove ads”. You pay once, through the App Store or Google Play. On a new phone, tap “Restore purchase” under it. The web version has no ads.

Does it need internet?

No. The app works offline and needs no account. The narrator’s voice and the privacy policy are inside the app. Only “Support”, at the foot of the rules, opens in your browser. It brings you to this page.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later, which means iPhone 8 and newer. Android 7 or later with an up-to-date Android System WebView (version 111 or newer). If the app opens on a page that asks for an update, update “Android System WebView” from Google Play and open the app again. On Android 7, 8 and 9, update “Chrome” instead.

How do I remove the names from the phone?

On the Players screen, hold the red list icon at the top (“Clear list”). It removes the list, the current deal and the game. The saved names stay: hold the red bin over them (“Forget all names”) to remove those too. Deleting the app removes everything. More in the privacy policy.
